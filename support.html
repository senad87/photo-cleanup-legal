Common questions

Does Photo Cleanup upload my photos?

No. Photo analysis and comparisons happen on your iPhone.

Does the app delete photos automatically?

No. You select what to remove and approve Apple’s system deletion confirmation.

How do I restore my lifetime purchase?

Open Photo Cleanup, choose Settings, and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I change photo access?

Open Photo Cleanup, choose Settings, and tap Manage Photo Access.
