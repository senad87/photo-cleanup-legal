Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Photo Cleanup is designed to analyze your photo library privately on your iPhone.

Photos and analysis

Photo Cleanup only accesses the photos you allow through Apple's photo-library permission. Visual fingerprints, similarity comparisons, face signals, and keeper recommendations are generated on your device. Photos and analysis results are not uploaded to our servers.

The app may cache analysis results locally on your device to make later scans faster. This local app data is removed when the app is deleted.

Photo Cleanup does not delete anything automatically. You choose the photos to remove and approve Apple's system deletion confirmation.

Purchases

Lifetime purchases are processed by Apple. Photo Cleanup checks the App Store for your purchase status but does not receive your payment-card or billing details.

Support

If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include in your message. We use it only to respond to your request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version and its effective date will always be available on this page.
